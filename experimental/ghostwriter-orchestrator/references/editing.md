# Editing facet

## Purpose and inputs

Repair demonstrated defects while preserving the article's argument and voice.
Receive the current draft, selected material, relevant sources, editorial
directions, and accepted findings. For a small passage, its original wording
and surrounding context may supply the necessary material without a formal
brief.

The prose owner decides which findings identify real problems. A reviewer is
not automatically right. Reject a suggested change when it would alter the
claim, remove a necessary condition, contradict the requested voice, or change
effective text for preference alone. Keep a short reason in the working audit
only when useful; do not narrate this adjudication in the article.

## Repair in this order

**Meaning first.** Restore missing required material and correct altered or
unsupported claims. Apply the correction to the claim itself. Do not keep a
false generalisation and append a qualification that contradicts it.

**Connections next.** Restore the missing relationship from the supplied
material, move the explanation to where it is needed, or remove an unjustified
transition. Do not invent a mechanism to make the paragraph sound coherent.

**Density and language next.** Read the surrounding text while changing the
smallest coherent passage. Name actors and actions, unpack nested conditions,
replace unnatural phrasing, clarify references, and remove redundant detours.
A structural defect may require changing a paragraph or section; minimal
editing does not mean fixing everything one word at a time.

**Final surface check.** Check grammar, punctuation, terminology, spelling
variant, quotation integrity, and source placement. When Vale is available or
requested, use [Vale surface review](vale-review.md): select the project
configuration or the appropriate bundled article profile, run the current
document, and adjudicate findings before editing. Recheck meaning after each
accepted change. Lint findings do not establish that the argument is correct.

## Preserve useful complexity

Do not simplify by deleting essential conditions, changing precise technical
terms into vague alternatives, or replacing a real mechanism with a slogan.
Do not force every sentence to be short. Cutting an entire irrelevant detour
may help more than shortening every sentence that expresses it.

Where there is a hard length limit, trim repeated points and optional examples
before compressing necessary explanations. If the required content cannot fit
without distortion, surface that constraint separately rather than quietly
sacrificing meaning.

## Regression check

For each changed passage, confirm that its claim, conditions, and evidence
still match the selected material. Reread the transition into and out of it.
Check that new wording has not leaked editorial discussion or reintroduced a
rejected point. Check cross-references elsewhere when definitions or section
order changed. Keep effective passages unchanged.

Do one targeted repair round and recheck it. A second round is available for
remaining concrete defects; do not run an automatic sequence of full rewrites.
Unresolved material issues must remain visible outside the prose. Further
iterations need a specific reason, not a demand to keep improving forever.

## Output

Return the revised text in the requested format. Return a diff or explanation
only when requested. Do not append a rating, editorial checklist, process
summary, or follow-up invitation to a requested article.
