# Briefing facet

## Purpose

Recover what the author means before turning it into prose. Produce a clean
writing packet, not a summary of everything discussed.

## Inputs

The current request, outline, notes, relevant conversation, supplied sources,
and any author-approved voice examples. Treat prior assistant suggestions as
proposals unless the user accepted them; silence is not acceptance. Do not
import unrelated personal context into the article.

## Separate substance from directions

Classify relevant source items as selected, replaced, excluded, or unresolved.
Keep their origin: author statement, source excerpt, or model proposal. Record
an item once; do not convert every sentence of a long chat into a formal record.

Selected material includes the thesis, claims, examples, mechanisms, evidence,
and conditions that change the meaning. Editorial directions include audience,
voice, structure, length, and exclusions. A sentence can contain both: split
"keep the capacity condition, but make it less academic" into a substantive
condition and a presentation instruction.

Use the latest clear author correction to update intent. It replaces the old
claim rather than becoming an extra disclaimer. A recent style request does
not erase an earlier substantive requirement. Conflicting factual sources are
not resolved simply by selecting whichever appeared last in the conversation.

"Selected" means selected for this draft, not necessarily approved by the
user or independently verified. Distinguish supplied fact, verified fact,
opinion, hypothesis, and illustration where that distinction matters.

## Create the writer's packet

Use [the brief template](../assets/writing-brief.md) as needed. Keep:

- The reader, purpose, scope, and requested deliverable.
- The selected claims with their essential conditions and source anchors.
- The argument's actual relationships: what supports, explains, qualifies, or
  follows from what. Mark an uncertain relationship rather than inventing it.
- Relevant examples and evidence, with exact wording for protected quotations.
  Where support is essential, record what it establishes and where it is needed.
  This may be an example, contrast, sequence, mechanism, or visual reference.
- Where sequence matters, a short reader-progression note: the question here,
  what is already established, what becomes clear, and why the next section
  follows. This is optional planning, not a mandatory article shape.
- A short editorial block and any supplied voice reference.

Write directions positively when possible. Replace "not academic" with
"name the people and actions; explain technical terms when first needed".
Replace "do not mention the rejected mentoring section" with the selected
positive scope. Keep necessary explicit prohibitions when positive scope
would not communicate a genuine requirement.

Do not include discarded drafts, rejected topics, review findings, or the
history of how the argument changed in the writing packet. Keep the
source/disposition record separately for the controller and fidelity reviewer,
using [the separate source-record template](../assets/source-record.md) when useful.

## Check the brief itself

Compare the relevant original notes with the packet in both directions. Each
material idea must be retained, explicitly excluded by scope, replaced by a
clear correction, or recorded as unresolved. Check examples, negations,
conditions, and emphasis, not merely whether the topic is mentioned.

An uncertainty in the author's argument is not always a defect: preserve it
when it belongs in the piece. Missing support for a factual claim is different.
Use supplied evidence, verify a load-bearing external claim when required, or
keep the unsupported claim out of a factual assertion. Do not turn "the author
said it" into "research establishes it".

Do not require a completed form before helping. For a small task the brief can
be a few lines. When information is missing, make conservative presentation
choices and continue where possible. Keep a genuinely blocking substantive gap
visible outside the article rather than guessing.

## Output

A clean writing packet and a separate source/disposition record. These are
working artifacts, not content to prepend to the article. Provide them to the
user only for brief-only mode, an explicit audit request, or a material gap.
