# Fidelity-review facet

## Purpose and inputs

Check that the draft says what the author intended, with the required evidence
and qualifications, and without importing the editorial conversation.
Receive the frozen draft, clean packet, relevant original notes and sources,
source/disposition record, and current editorial directions. Do not receive
the other reviewer's verdict or the writer's defence of the draft.

## Check both transformations

Check original material to brief, then brief to draft. Do not assume the
briefing step was lossless. If a note is absent from the brief, determine
whether it was deliberately excluded, clearly superseded, or accidentally
lost. A summariser's unexplained omission is not permission to omit an idea.

For each material claim, example, and condition, locate its treatment in the
draft. A keyword match is not coverage: the draft must express the intended
relationship and qualification. Where the brief marks explanatory support as
essential, check that its job survives; a topic mention is not an explanation. Briefly record a missing or changed item with
its source anchor. Do not require a sentence-by-sentence provenance table for
ordinary connective prose.

Then check the reverse direction: identify substantive draft claims that the
sources do not support. Distinguish a permitted illustrative example or
transparent inference from an invented fact or silently added causal premise.

## Meaning that must survive

Check negation, scope, actors, timing, frequency, quantities, comparisons, uncertainty,
and conditions. In particular, do not let "may" become "will", an observation
become causation, or an author opinion become a research finding. Preserve the
actual strength of the source, not an unnecessarily weak version of it.

Frequency and certainty are separate: a recurring observation is stronger than
a statement that something is possible, without being universal or guaranteed.
Preserve supplied frequency or prevalence qualifiers, including qualitative
ones. Cautious wording must not turn an observed pattern into a hypothetical
event; retain the qualifier without inventing numerical rates.
Check introduced qualifiers too: plausibility is not support for a claim about
how common or rare something is. Keep a new frequency or prevalence qualifier
only when the selected material supports it; otherwise remove that added
premise rather than replace it with another unsupported modifier.

Preserve quotations exactly unless the task explicitly authorises paraphrase;
mark paraphrases as such rather than retaining quotation marks. Check that a
citation supports the clause it accompanies. Distinguish author-provided facts
from independently checked facts. Do not claim verification without inspecting
a suitable source. Use available research tools when verification is required;
do not expand the article with unrelated research.

## Two kinds of conversation leakage

Direct leakage includes references to this drafting session, directions to the
writer, revision summaries, working labels, claim IDs, and review findings.

Indirect leakage includes a rejected idea resurrected as a disclaimer, a
comparison introduced solely to rebut earlier feedback, or a writing direction
recast as a claim about the subject.

For each suspected leak, identify why it serves the editing exchange rather
than the intended reader. A useful scope statement is not automatically a
leak. An article about writing can legitimately discuss editorial notes.
Phrase blacklists can suggest places to inspect; they cannot decide this.

## Output and limits

Return exact, source-anchored findings using [the findings template](../assets/review-findings.md).
Separate missing material, altered meaning, unsupported additions, leakage,
and evidence gaps. Note actual source checks and unresolved factual questions.
Do not rewrite the article, add new requirements, or report a global quality
score. A review can find no issues; that is not proof of factual completeness.

When the author's selected claim conflicts with reliable evidence, identify
the conflict. Do not silently change the thesis or publish a known falsehood
in the name of fidelity. Return the smallest factual decision that needs
resolution, separately from the draft.
