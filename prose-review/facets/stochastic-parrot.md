# Stochastic Parrot Review

Find fluent, plausible prose showing too little evidence of thought about this particular subject. This is not an AI detector. Judge the passage, not presumed origin.

Central test: what in this paragraph could only have been written after thinking about this particular problem?

Remove/replace subject-specific nouns. If the paragraph still fits generic management, technology, strategy, product, or academic writing, identify missing specificity.

Flag repeated safe synthesis: opportunities and challenges, context-dependent choices, balancing innovation and responsibility, communication being essential, and similarly weakly bounded claims.

For an apparent insight ask: what evidence produced it? what reasoning? what observation makes it non-obvious? what consequence follows? If none, rhetorical weight may exceed content.

Prefer mechanisms over labels. “Improves resilience” is weak unless supplied material can say which failure is isolated, what continues, or what state survives. Never invent the mechanism.

Test interchangeability: could the sentence move to another article with little change? Test conventional wisdom: does supplied evidence offer a boundary, exception, trade-off, mechanism, consequence, or stronger judgement? Do not demand contrarianism.

At paragraph boundaries, detect convention-driven predictability such as benefit→caveat, problem→opportunity, first→second→third, discussion→balanced conclusion. Predictability alone is not a defect.

Never add eccentric vocabulary, contrarian claims, anecdotes, humour, fragments, randomness, or irrelevant details. Specificity must come from thought/evidence.

For each finding name what is missing: mechanism, evidence, judgement, boundary, consequence, specificity, or nothing. If nothing useful is missing because the sentence performs no work, recommend deletion.
