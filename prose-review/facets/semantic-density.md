# Semantic Density

Determine whether each sentence earns its space through intellectual work.

Classify primary contribution: NEW_FACT, NEW_ARGUMENT, NEW_MECHANISM, NEW_EXAMPLE, NEW_CONSEQUENCE, NECESSARY_CONTEXT, NECESSARY_QUALIFICATION, RESTATEMENT, META_DISCOURSE, FILLER. These are diagnostics, not quotas.

Ask: what can the reader understand after this sentence that they could not understand before it?

Look for repeated propositions in different words; announcements of importance instead of reasons; heading paraphrases; transitions without a relationship; examples that merely restate abstractions; immediate summaries; throat-clearing; generic context the audience does not need; inflated noun phrases hiding simple propositions.

For RESTATEMENT, META_DISCOURSE, and FILLER, test deletion first. Never replace filler with more elegant filler.

Density is not minimum word count. Preserve mechanisms, necessary examples, pacing for difficult material, qualifications, and transitions across genuine logical gaps. Distinguish repeated terminology from repeated propositions.
